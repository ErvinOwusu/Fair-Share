About Us

We are five college students looking to create a helpful tool for people living with roommates. Our goal is to help people track shared expenses so everybody can pay their FairShare.

How we got Started

This is a course project for CSCI-4440 Software Design and Documentation at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. Our goal is to give roommates one place to track shared expenses and house supplies.
